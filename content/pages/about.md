# About

Hi, I'm **Paulo Cabral**, a database engineer specialized in **PostgreSQL**.

**Cabral's Log** is where I write down what I learn in the field: query tuning,
replication, vacuum, and the internals behind them. I also cover the rest of the
data landscape I work with, including **DynamoDB**, **ElastiCache**, and **AWS**
data services.

## Why "Cabral's Log"?

Every navigator keeps a logbook, and every database keeps a log.
This blog is a bit of both.
